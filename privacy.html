Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Who this covers

This policy explains what information The Lifting School LLC collects, how it is used, and how you can have it removed. If you have any questions, contact info@theliftingschool.com. Because this is a technical document that needs to be accurate and up to date, I have used Claude AI to write and review this policy.

What I don't do

  • I don't sell, rent, or trade your information. Ever.
  • I don't run ads, and I don't use your information for ads.
  • This website has no analytics, no tracking scripts, and no cookies.
  • I don't embed third-party widgets such as social media buttons or video players, which can track you. Links to other sites are plain links, and those sites only learn about you if you click.
  • I don't share your information with anyone outside my business, except the services that handle it for me, which are listed below.

Google account data

I run private scheduling and record-keeping software for my coaching business. With my own Google account's permission, it accesses:

  • Google Calendar events: read, and create or update, to schedule training sessions and invite clients.
  • Google Sheets contents: read only, to keep each client's training program history.
  • Google Drive file names and modified dates: read only, to find those spreadsheets.

How that data is used

It is used only to run my coaching business: scheduling sessions, sending reminders, and keeping client training history. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone outside my business.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where it is stored

Copies of this data are stored on computers I own, on a private network that is not reachable from the internet.

Removing your data

To have your information deleted, email info@theliftingschool.com. I can also revoke my software's access to my Google account at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This website

This website saves nothing about you just for visiting it. The only time it handles your information is when you use the sign-up form, and the services below keep their own records.

  • Sign-up form: if you fill it out, what you enter (name, email, phone, city, state, age, gender, height, weight, joint pain, goals, equipment, schedule preferences, and any notes) is sent through Formspree, a form service, to my email. Formspree stores submissions on its servers under its own privacy policy. I use your answers only to contact you about training and to build your program, and I will delete them if you ask.
  • Hosting: this site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses to operate its service, under its own privacy statement. I don't use those logs.
