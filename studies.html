    Study Type
    Meta-analysis
    Subjects
    Humans
    Sex

    A systematic review and meta-analysis of 51 studies with 6,434 participants compared body composition and fitness in transgender and cisgender people. Transgender women had more lean mass than cisgender women but similar fat mass, strength and peak aerobic fitness.     A meta-regression of 67 studies with 2,058 participants examined how weekly set volume and training frequency relate to muscle growth and strength.
