Having Gordon Ramsay's recipe for his famous beef wellington is no guarantee that you can pull off the dish. This is why I named my company The Lifting School. Not only do I want to teach you how to do things properly, but I want you to understand why something is more or less effective.

Time and Consistency

The less time you have available, the more important it is that no effort is wasted. You will experience the advantage of a professionally written program more than anyone, so long as you are willing to bring the intensity to your weekly sessions.

I've worked with several clients who could only commit to 30-60 minutes a week, and they were always astounded by how consistently their strength improved. That said, if your goals are not compatible with the amount of time you have available, I will let you know.

I am happy to include workouts that require minimal equipment, or use what's available in a hotel gym, if you let me know what you have to work with.

If you are planning an extended trip (4+ weeks), and do not plan to workout during that period, give me a couple weeks' notice and I will pause your training while you're gone.

While I do not expect perfect consistency, and will not give you a hard time for missing some of your workouts, I also will not extend our training period just because you decided to skip a week or two.

Payment and Privacy

I handle all my payments through Stripe invoices, which are sent to your email. You don't need to sign up for any accounts, and every common form of payment is accepted.

It's important to me that you never feel locked into long-term training before you know what it's like to work with me, so I never offer more than 12 weeks' worth of training to new clients.

If something unexpected comes up before your package ends, say a medical emergency that leaves you unable to train for some time, I'm happy to pause and extend our training for however long you're out.

Ending our working relationship is as simple as telling me you don't plan to renew, and then I'm happy to share final recommendations and advice to ensure your continued success going forward!
