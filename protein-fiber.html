About These Numbers

The low end is enough to maintain and build muscle for most people. Higher intakes lower the chance that you will lose muscle during a fat loss phase and maximize your results from hypertrophy workouts. Research suggests that anything beyond 0.8 grams per pound is unlikely to carry additional benefit.

Fiber targets are derived from a rough estimation of daily calorie expenditure based on your bodyweight, erring on the side of more. Gradually increase your intake to minimize the chances of stomach issues, as a sudden spike in fiber intake can cause bloating, indigestion, etc.
